Part B- Prevention Checklist:

🛡️ Stay Safe Online – Prevention Checklist

✅ General Safety Measures

* Always verify the sender’s identity before clicking on any email or message link.
* Carefully check website URLs for correct spelling and secure connection (https://).
* Never share sensitive information such as passwords, OTPs, or bank details with anyone.
* Use strong and unique passwords for different online accounts.
* Enable two-factor authentication (2FA) to add an extra layer of security.
* Avoid accessing important accounts on public or unsecured Wi-Fi networks.

💰 UPI / Financial Safety Measures

* Never approve unknown or suspicious payment requests on UPI applications.
* Do not scan QR codes received from unknown or untrusted sources.
* Always double-check the receiver’s details before making any payment.

 🚨 Reporting Cybercrime

* Report any cybercrime incident on the official portal: https://cybercrime.gov.in
* Contact the Cyber Crime Helpline: 1930 (available 24x7 across India)

 ⚠️ Final Note

Being aware and cautious while using the internet is the most effective way to prevent cybercrime and protect personal information.
